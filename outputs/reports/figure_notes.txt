Figure notes and tabulated values
=================================

purpose
-------
  Figures carry no in-panel annotation. Values that would otherwise be
  printed inside an axes are recorded here. Every panel is also
  available as CSV under outputs/data.

figure 5, damage regimes
------------------------
  Spall radius of the 12 cm plate rises from 1.75 m at alpha = 1e-05
  to 5.70 m at alpha = 2.73e-02. Crush radius of the same plate falls
  from 3.37 m to 2.51 m. Plate thicknesses are realised to within one
  skeleton cell; see the log printed by fig05_regimes.py.
  d (m) | R_spall at alpha min | at alpha max
   0.06 |                0.000 |        1.536
   0.09 |                0.000 |        4.096
   0.12 |                1.747 |        5.701
   0.15 |                2.894 |        6.623
