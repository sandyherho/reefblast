Symbols, units, and reference values
====================================

units
-----
  All quantities are SI. Figures 2 and 8(a) use P and theta as units
  of pressure and time; Figure 4 and animation 3 scale by R0, p0 and
  t_c = R0 sqrt(rho/p0). Figure 7 and animation 1 use an arbitrary
  source amplitude and show only ratios.

reference values (illustrative, not calibrated)
-----------------------------------------------
  rho_l = 1025.0 kg m^-3, c_l = 1500.0 m s^-1, K_l = 2.3062e+09 Pa
  p0 = 151601.25 Pa (canopy at 5.0 m), kappa = 1.4
  rho_s = 1600.0 kg m^-3, c_s = 3000.0 m s^-1, Z_s = 4.8000e+06 Pa s m^-1
  sigma_t = 2.0 MPa, sigma_c = 20.0 MPa
  similitude: K_P = 52.16 MPa, A_P = 1.13, K_T = 92.5 us kg^-1/3, A_T = 0.22
  W = 1.0 kg TNT equivalent, canopy above plate h = 0.15 m, reference plate d = 0.12 m
  plate family d = [0.06, 0.09, 0.12, 0.15] m
  diel geometry: charge at 3.5 m, plates at 5.0 m; alpha_max = [0.001, 0.01, 0.03]
  time steps per smallest decay constant: 100
  bubble: R0 = 0.5 mm, sigma = 0.072 N m^-1, mu = 1e-3 Pa s

provenance
----------
  The similitude constants are the TNT values widely tabulated after
  Cole (1948); improvised ammonium-nitrate charges enter only through
  an equivalent W. Skeleton density, sound speed and strengths are
  round values chosen to sit in a plausible range for porous
  aragonite; they are not taken from a specific measurement and every
  threshold result scales with them. The canopy void fraction is a
  free parameter.

what the results do not depend on
---------------------------------
  The impulse invariant and the onset criterion Z_c/Z_s = tanh(delta)
  hold for any values of the material constants. The crossover p*
  depends only on alpha and K_l.
